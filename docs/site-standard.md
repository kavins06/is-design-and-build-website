# What "finished" means for this site

The owner's brief, 2026-11-12: a customer-facing, production-grade marketing site that makes a homeowner want to hire I.S Design + Build. Every change is judged against this page.

## The job of the site

A homeowner in the DC area arrives, sees work they would want in their own home, believes this firm can deliver it, and gets in touch. Everything on a page either builds that desire and trust or moves the visitor to enquire. Anything else is removed.

## The owner's verdict on the previous build

- The home page scroll and feel are good. Keep them.
- Too many words. It reads like an article, not a marketing site.
- The copy is hedged and odd. It does not sound like a firm worth hiring.
- The interior pages and menu do not share the home page's look. They are text documents.
- SEO material was put on the page unfiltered. The SEO is needed, but not as a content dump.

## Rules

### Show, then say
1. Every page opens on a full-screen photograph, the same dark, drawn, architectural look as the home page.
2. Every screen of scrolling has a picture, a drawing or one large statement. No screen is only paragraphs.
3. One idea per section. A heading, at most two sentences, one action.

### Words
1. Write like a confident builder talking to a homeowner: short, plain, specific.
2. No hedging ("may", "can help you consider", "one option to explore") unless the fact really is uncertain.
3. No filler sections that exist only to hold keywords.
4. Word budget: home page under 350 visible words before the footer, labels included; a service page under 200 words above its questions.
5. Never invent facts: no prices, timelines, licences, awards, guarantees or project details the owner has not confirmed.

### SEO without the dump
1. Keywords live in the title, description, H1, one lede sentence, image alt text, internal links and structured data.
2. Longer answers that searchers need go in a questions section that is closed by default. The text stays in the page for search engines and for visitors who want it.
3. Each service keeps its own page, and old URLs keep redirecting.
4. Location terms (Washington, DC, Northern Virginia, Montgomery County, Bethesda, Chevy Chase) appear once per page in natural copy, not in lists repeated on every section.

### Conversion
1. One primary action everywhere: start a project. The phone number is always one tap away.
2. Proof near every ask: real project photographs, years in business, Google reviews.
3. The enquiry form must actually deliver the enquiry, confirm it, and fail visibly if it cannot.

### Phone first, not phone as an afterthought
1. Every page is designed and checked separately at phone width, as a phone user would use it: thumb reach, one column, text readable without zooming.
2. The first screen on a phone shows the headline and the main action without scrolling.
3. Phones get their own patterns where the desktop one fights the thumb: a full-screen menu, swipe galleries instead of pinned sideways scrolling, and a fixed Call / Start a project bar.
4. Tap targets are at least 44px. Nothing scrolls sideways by accident.

### Production grade
1. Works on phone, tablet and desktop in Chrome, Safari and Firefox.
2. Accessible to WCAG 2.1 AA: contrast, keyboard use, focus, alt text, reduced motion.
3. Fast: sized images, lazy media, no layout shift, fonts preloaded.
4. No dead ends: no broken links, placeholder text, concept pages or stock labels left visible.
5. Brand complete: logo, favicon, share image, consistent name, phone, address and hours.

## Still needed from the owner

- Licence and insurance details, and lead-paint certification, if they are to be claimed.
- Confirmation that office hours are Monday to Friday.
- Where form enquiries should be delivered.
